# Neurotal
NeuroTal — The Prefrontal Exchange

"Focus is the new Gold. Discipline is the only Currency."

What is NeuroTal? NeuroTal is a behavioral finance platform that transforms human focus and cognitive discipline into a live market economy. The system treats attention, impulse control, consistency, and deep work as measurable economic assets — visualized as a real-time trading exchange where your behavior directly affects your personal market value. This is not a productivity app. It is not a habit tracker. It is a competitive cognitive exchange.

The Problem Modern attention economy platforms (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts) are engineered to exploit the limbic system — the impulsive, dopamine-seeking part of the brain. Advice does not work. Willpower does not scale. People change because of incentives, status, competition, and market pressure. NeuroTal weaponizes those same mechanics — but redirects them toward self-control.

The Dual Asset System $PREFRONTAL — Blue-Chip Cognitive Asset Represents discipline, sustained focus, executive control, and delayed gratification.

Earned through uninterrupted deep work sessions Scarce, difficult to mint, long-term value oriented Powers elite access, reputation score, and high-trust networking

$LIMBIC — Volatile Degeneration Asset Represents impulsiveness, dopamine-seeking, distraction, and chaotic consumption.

Generated automatically through doomscrolling and app-switching Inflationary, unstable, emotionally reactive Functions as the market's contrast layer and behavioral mirror

Reputation System Your profile displays a dynamically calculated badge based on your $PREFRONTAL / $LIMBIC ratio. Core Mechanics Proof of Focus (PoF) The system tracks:

Sustained task engagement Session consistency and endurance Low task-switching behavior

Behavioral Detection Cognition is inferred through:

App activity and screen interaction Scrolling patterns and session duration Context switching frequency Optional biometric integrations

Anti-Exploit Philosophy Key challenges: fake focus farming, automation, behavior spoofing. Required infrastructure:

Anomaly detection and trust scoring Adaptive behavioral models Anti-bot infrastructure Behavioral consistency validation

Ethical Commitments NeuroTal acknowledges potential risks and commits to avoiding:

Humiliation mechanics without a recovery path Psychological coercion or predatory monetization Self-worth permanently tied to market value Addiction to the ranking system itself

The platform is designed to redirect competitive instincts toward self-improvement — not to exploit vulnerability.
